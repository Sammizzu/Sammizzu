## About Me

I am an international education professional and researcher with a PhD in Educational Management from **Chulalongkorn University, Thailand**.

My academic and professional experience spans international student affairs, higher education administration, Chinese language education, cross-cultural learning, and educational program development.

My research explores how universities can foster global leadership competencies through effective student affairs management and evidence-informed educational strategies.

## Featured Research Project

### Global Leadership Competencies in International Higher Education

**Doctoral Research | Chulalongkorn University**

My doctoral research investigates student affairs management strategies for developing global leadership competencies among international undergraduate students in Beijing, China.

The study integrates competency framework development, quantitative research, instrument validation, needs assessment, environmental analysis, and strategic planning.
